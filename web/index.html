    Pay Dirt
    
      
    Three tunnels. One has gold.
    
      
    The miner picks a tunnel. Then someone knocks on one of the other two and it caves in. Does he stay, or switch to the last one?
    
      
    It’s the famous Monty Hall puzzle, where the answer is usually “switch.” Here it depends on who knocked:
    
      
      
        
    • Tommy is friendly. He knows where the gold is and knocks on an empty tunnel. Switch.
      • 
        
    • The Creaks is just old wood settling. It tells you nothing. Coin flip.
      • 
        
    • The Joker only knocks when you already picked the gold, hoping you’ll leave. Stay.
      • 
      
    
      
    The Rookie is a small AI. Nobody tells it any of this. It learns by watching, live in this tab.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Who’s knocking?
    
      
    
      
    Old Timer’s guess for this part of the mine. He knows all three spirits but not which one is here, so he listens to every knock.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Scoreboard 
    
      
    
      
      
        
    • Old Timer knows every spirit’s trick. The best anyone can do.
      • 
        
    • Rookie is the AI, learning as it goes.
      • 
        
    • Bookworm always switches, because that’s what the book says.
      • 
      
    
      
    Everyone hears the same knock. Only their choice is different. Missed is gold lost per 100 picks by not knowing who knocked.
