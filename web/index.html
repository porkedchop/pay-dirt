Who’s Knocking

Old Cornish miners swore spirits knocked on the walls. Some led you to ore, some messed with you, and some were just creaky timbers. Every knock here is a Monty Hall door, and “always switch” only works if whoever knocked actually knows where the ore is. The Rookie is a tiny AI (710 numbers) learning live in this tab. Nobody tells it the rules. It has to figure out who’s knocking on its own.
